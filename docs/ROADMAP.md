# What I want to do next

My own list, in the order it matters to me. Not a plan anyone should hold me
to.

1. Tag drops in the light table while culling. `./pl learn` needs twelve of
   a reason before it will train one, and the reasons are the only signal it
   cannot get from anywhere else. The whole corpus still has three frames
   labelled "my shadow" across 548.
2. Place one control point in PhotoLab and move the tint slider once, so
   locals and tint can be written into the sidecars.
3. Shoot the next card and bench it before touching any threshold. Keep the
   RAWs until you have looked at its answer key once. The 98 loose frames
   `./pl evaluate` names as a smoke test have no answer key at all; an hour
   in the light table turns them into a fifth shoot the bench can use.
4. Keep public releases reproducible. Version 0.1.4 was published on
   2026-09-24 with a signed, notarized installer and the OpenCV wheels used
   to build it. Follow RELEASING.md for each update, including the source,
   repository metadata, release notes and bundled dependency checks.
5. The animal gate lets 29 heads through. Of the 3,671 annotated Oxford-IIIT
   Pet images, 1,601 heads read as a main human face and 29 get past both
   witnesses. Eight of the 29 are landmark-read, the only state in which a
   fault can veto, and none of the eight carries one today, so this is a
   margin rather than a live fault. The gate cannot move to close it: pet
   heads read animal at p5 = 0.187 and my own faces at p99 = 0.765. A third
   opinion before a frame is binned on a face is the fix if one is wanted.
6. The blink rule's misses that are not the smile gate: on CEW's 1,192
   closed-eye crops, 133 are the landmarker not seeing shut eyes at all. That
   is the measurement failing rather than the rule.
7. The slow ones, which all need the data the first three produce: sharpness
   on animals, bursts that understand pose, a shadow detector that works.
